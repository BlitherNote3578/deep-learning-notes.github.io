# Deep Learning Notes

Hi, welcome to my deep learning notes on the course: [Practical Deep Learning for Coders](https://course.fast.ai/).

In this repository, I document what I learn throughout the course, what my thoughts and ideas are and the projects I work on as part of the course.
This blog mainly serves as a mental backup for myself, so I can come back later and refresh specific concepts without having to take the entire course again.

At the same time, I hope this blog can be useful for others who find it easier to understand complex topics by learning alongside someone else. I try to write down my thought process and reasoning step by step, for why I have written down what.
